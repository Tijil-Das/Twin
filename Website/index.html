Capabilities

Everything Google Drive should have been.

Designed to replace the confusing, resource-heavy official client.

Complete Process Visibility

Know exactly what is syncing at every moment. Real byte progress bars, calculated transfer speeds, and plain error messages—no mysterious spinning wheels or stuck files.

Byte Accurate · Zero Guesswork

More Features & Freedom

Sync any folder on any drive (C:, D:, or external SSDs). Browse and restore past file versions, recover trash, and automatically export Google Docs into offline Word and Excel files.

Any Folder · Revisions Vault

Ultra-Lightweight & Silent

Uses 95% less RAM than Google Drive. Built-in hardware governor pauses syncing when you're gaming or compiling (CPU > 70%) or on low battery (< 20%) so your PC stays fast.

28 MB RAM · Auto CPU Pause
